Copyright (C) 2026 顾文斌

FreePark is open source software released under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)](LICENSE). This document states the **ethical conditions** that the author expects every user and organization to follow. It expresses why this project exists and who should **not** benefit from it.

FreePark 在 [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) 下开源发布。本文档说明作者对使用者的**伦理期望**：本项目希望帮助尊重劳动者的人与组织，**不**希望被剥削劳动者、损害工人权益的主体所使用。

## Our stance / 我们的立场

We believe software should serve people fairly. Parking infrastructure should not be built on the suffering of workers.

我们主张技术应公平地服务社会。停车与场站管理系统的价值，不应建立在压榨劳动者之上。

## Prohibited practices / 反对的行为

The following practices are **incompatible** with the spirit of FreePark. Organizations that **currently engage in** any of the following **may not use, deploy, operate, or offer** this software (including modified versions) in any production, commercial, or operational capacity:

下列行为与 FreePark 的发布目的相悖。**正在实施**以下任一做法的组织或个人，**不得**以生产、商业或运营方式使用、部署、运行或对外提供本软件（含修改版本）：

### 1. “996” and exploitative overtime / 反对 996 与剥削性加班

- Mandatory or systemic **long-hour culture** (e.g. “996”: 9:00–21:00, six days a week) or equivalent arrangements that routinely violate labor laws or reasonable rest standards.
- Treating extreme overtime as a default expectation rather than an exceptional, compensated, and lawful measure.

- 强制或系统性的**超长工时文化**（例如「996」：早 9 点至晚 9 点、每周 6 天），或实质上违反劳动法、合理休息标准的类似安排。
- 将严重超时加班作为常态，而非在合法、补偿到位前提下的例外情况。

### 2. Unequal pay for equal work / 反对同工不同酬

- **Paying different compensation for substantially the same work** on the basis of gender, ethnicity, region, identity, or other unjustified factors—not bona fide role, skill, or performance differences.
- Policies or practices that systematically deny **equal pay for equal work**.

- 在**工作内容实质相同**的情况下，因性别、民族、地域、身份等不合理因素支付不同报酬（合理的岗位、技能、绩效差异除外）。
- 系统性落实或纵容**同工不同酬**的制度或做法。

### 3. Exploitative labor outsourcing / 反对损害工人权益的人力外包

- Using **labor outsourcing**, subcontracting, or “dispatch” arrangements primarily to **avoid employer responsibilities**, weaken worker protections, or deny direct employment rights (social insurance, fair contracts, collective bargaining, safe working conditions).
- Treating outsourced workers as disposable labor without comparable legal and social protections.

- 以**人力外包**、劳务派遣、分包等方式，**主要目的**为规避用人单位责任、削弱劳动者保障、拒绝直接用工应承担的义务（社保、公平合同、集体协商、安全劳动条件等）。
- 将外包劳动者视为可随时替换的劳动力，且不提供对等的法律与社会保障。

## Who may not use this software / 不得使用本软件的主体

Any **enterprise, government body, contractor, vendor, or individual** that **practices** any of the above—or **knowingly profits** from such practices in its core operations—**is not permitted** to use FreePark.

任何在核心业务中**实施**上述行为，或**明知并从中获利**的企业、政府机构、承包商、服务商或个人，**均不得使用** FreePark。

This includes using FreePark internally, reselling it as a service, embedding it in products, or operating parking sites powered by this code.

上述限制涵盖：内部使用、作为服务转售、嵌入产品，或以本代码支撑停车场/场站运营等一切用途。

## Who we welcome / 我们欢迎的使用者

- Individuals and organizations that **respect labor laws** and **fair employment**.
- Communities, public-interest projects, and businesses that pay fairly and take direct responsibility for their workers.
- Contributors who help improve parking for everyone without exploiting those who build and maintain the system.

- 尊重劳动法、实行**公平用工**的个人与组织。
- 合理付酬、对劳动者承担直接责任的社区、公益项目与企业。
- 愿意改进停车体验、同时不剥削建设与运维者的贡献者。
